Our story

Experience. Passion. Drive.

Grootfontein Auto Electric was established in 2016. The business was originally started by Willy alongside a business partner. In 2024, Willy took over the business independently, continuing to build Grootfontein Auto Electric around his experience and passion for automotive electrical work.

Today, with Antje by his side, the business continues to grow while keeping the same commitment to quality workmanship and personal service.

Built on experience. Driven by passion.Willy's passion for cars goes beyond the workshop, with a love for rally and oval racing that keeps him close to the heart of motorsport.
